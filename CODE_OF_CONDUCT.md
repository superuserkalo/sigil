# Code of conduct

Sigil is a place for players, mod makers and developers to work on a tool together. Everyone is
welcome, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to modding or to open source.
- Assume good intent, and disagree about ideas, not people.
- Keep feedback specific and constructive.
- Respect mod creators: credit their work and link to it rather than reposting it.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery aimed at a person, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Posting someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, trolling, or repeatedly derailing a discussion.

## Reporting a problem

If someone's behaviour bothers you, use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment or issue,
or open an issue saying you want to raise a conduct problem privately and the maintainer will
contact you. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions, and block people who break these
rules, in the project's issues, pull requests and other spaces.
