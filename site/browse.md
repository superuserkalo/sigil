# Browse and install Deadlock mods from GameBanana

> Browse GameBanana's Deadlock mods inside Sigil, search on your own PC with typo tolerance, and install into a free slot with the mod switched off.

Sigil reads GameBanana's Deadlock catalog live, so you can find a mod, read its page and install it without a browser or an unzip step.

![Sigil's Browse page with a trending banner, a top ten for the week and a row of rising mods.](https://sigilmods.app/img/hero-1200.webp)

## Finding a mod

The landing page shows the week's top mods, trending rails, a Browse by hero rail and featured picks. Tabs split mods, sounds and work in progress. On the left you can pick a category, a subcategory or a hero; a hero pick also finds that hero's sounds and HUD work.

Filters cover NSFW content, outdated mods, whether a mod is already installed and featured mods. Sort by newest, recently updated, most liked, most viewed, most downloaded or name, and limit results to a date window. Active filters show as chips you can remove one at a time. Favorites are saved on your PC, and mods or creators you hide stay out of the way until you bring them back.

## Search that runs on your PC

Sigil downloads a public index of the catalog and matches your search on your own computer. It forgives typos, copes with words written together or apart and knows what many mods are based on, so typing a game or series name finds skins whose title never mentions it. What you type is not sent anywhere.

![A search for "chainsaw man" finding the skin "Asa Mitaka over Paige", whose title does not name the series.](https://sigilmods.app/img/search.webp)

You can also paste a GameBanana link or id into the search to open that mod, or paste several links at once to queue them.

## Installing

1. Press Install on a card, or open the mod and choose a file.
2. Sigil downloads the archive, lists the mod files inside and checks what it is about to write. If the mod contains programs or scripts, the review says so before anything is installed.
3. Choose the file or variant you want and confirm. Sigil puts it in the first free slot.

New installs land switched off by default, so nothing changes in your game until you turn the mod on in Installed. You can change that in Settings, Mods. A mod you already have shows Update or Replace instead, and an update keeps its slot and your on or off choice.

Mods on GameBanana belong to their creators. Opening a mod shows its pictures, description, files, changelog and a link to the creator's page.
